────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
1. SCOPE
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
This policy governs data handling within RaPaX™.

RaPaX™ is a self-hosted software platform.
This policy applies to all tiers and versions of RaPaX™.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
2. NO DATA COLLECTION BY ARCHER CHAIN ANALYTICS™
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Archer Chain Analytics™ does not collect any data
through RaPaX™.

RaPaX™ does not:
- Transmit data to Archer Chain Analytics™ or any
  third party controlled by Archer Chain Analytics™.
- Maintain any external database, server, or cloud service.
- Record installation events or operational data externally.
- Collect telemetry, analytics, or usage metrics.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
3. NO TELEMETRY
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
RaPaX™ contains no telemetry.

No analytics, crash reporting, feature tracking, or usage
monitoring is implemented or transmitted.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
4. NO EXTERNAL TRANSMISSION
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
RaPaX™ does not initiate outbound connections except:

- To blockchain provider APIs you explicitly configure
  (BlockCypher, Infura, Alchemy, Helius, or your node).
- To the AcerbE™ fingerprinting service you configure.

All other operations are executed locally on your system.
All transaction data, buyer data, and audit records
remain on your infrastructure.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
5. LOCAL DATA — YOUR RESPONSIBILITY
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
RaPaX™ stores the following locally on your system:

- rapax.db — SQLite database containing product catalog,
  transaction records, payment addresses, fingerprint
  metadata, delivery records, and audit logs.
- storage/products/ — Uploaded product files.
- storage/fingerprinted/ — AcerbE™-processed output files.
- logs/ — Exported audit reports.
- .env — Configuration including operator credentials
  and blockchain API keys.

You are solely responsible for:
- Security of all locally stored data.
- Encryption of the database at rest if required.
- Compliance with PIPEDA and applicable privacy legislation
  regarding any personal data you collect from Buyers.
- Lawful handling of Buyer wallet addresses and identifiers.
- Backup and recovery of all operational data.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
6. BUYER DATA — OPERATOR OBLIGATION
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
If you collect Buyer information (wallet address,
email, transaction history) through RaPaX™, you are
the data controller.

Archer Chain Analytics™ is not the data controller for
any Buyer information you process using RaPaX™.

You must:
- Maintain a compliant privacy policy for your own Buyers.
- Obtain lawful basis for processing Buyer personal data.
- Respond to data subject requests from your Buyers.
- Disclose to Buyers what data you collect and retain.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
7. OPERATOR CREDENTIALS
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
RaPaX™ generates and stores:
- Operator JWT secret (in .env)
- Operator authentication tokens (session-based)
- Blockchain provider API keys (in .env)
- AcerbE™ API key (in .env)

You are solely responsible for the security of these
credentials. Archer Chain Analytics™ has no access to
or knowledge of your credentials.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
8. THIRD-PARTY BLOCKCHAIN PROVIDERS
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
RaPaX™ communicates with blockchain APIs you configure.

These providers have their own privacy policies:
- BlockCypher: blockcypher.com/privacy
- Infura: infura.io/privacy
- Alchemy: alchemy.com/privacy
- Helius: helius.dev/privacy

Transaction data submitted to these APIs is subject
to their respective policies. You are responsible for
reviewing and accepting those policies.

9. UPDATES TO THIS POLICY
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Archer Chain Analytics™ may update this policy with
new versions of RaPaX™. Updates will be distributed
with the updated software package.
