1. PURPOSE
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
This disclosure defines what RaPaX™ is, what it does,
and what it does not do.

It is provided to protect buyers, operators, and Archer
Chain Analytics™ from false claims, misrepresentation,
and misuse.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
2. OPERATIONAL SCOPE
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
RaPaX™ is a self-hosted, sovereign digital product
vending platform.

RaPaX™ operates entirely on your own infrastructure.
RaPaX™ does not connect to Archer Chain Analytics™ servers.
RaPaX™ does not require any external platform account.
RaPaX™ does not collect, transmit, or store any data
externally.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
3. PAYMENT PROCESSING
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
RaPaX™ facilitates cryptocurrency payments by:

- Generating unique payment addresses per transaction.
- Monitoring the blockchain for payment confirmation
  via your configured provider APIs (BlockCypher,
  Infura, Alchemy, Helius, or your own nodes).
- Triggering the delivery pipeline upon confirmed payment.

RaPaX™ does NOT:

- Hold, custody, or transmit cryptocurrency funds.
- Act as a payment processor, money transmitter,
  or financial intermediary.
- Guarantee transaction confirmation times.
- Guarantee pricing stability of any cryptocurrency.

You are solely responsible for regulatory compliance
in your jurisdiction regarding cryptocurrency receipts.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
4. ASSET DELIVERY
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
RaPaX™ delivers digital assets via:

- Cryptographically random, time-limited download tokens.
- No direct file paths are ever exposed to buyers.
- All tokens expire per your configured TTL.
- All delivery events are immutably logged.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
5. AcerbE™ INTEGRATION
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
RaPaX™ integrates with AcerbE™ for asset fingerprinting.

When AcerbE™ is configured:
- Each asset is fingerprinted with buyer identity,
  transaction ID, and timestamp before delivery.
- Fingerprinted files are stored separately from originals.
- All fingerprint metadata is logged in the Audit Log.

RaPaX™ includes a development fallback fingerprint for
use when AcerbE™ is not reachable. This fallback is NOT
suitable for production use and does not provide the
cryptographic protections of AcerbE™.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
6. AUDIT LOGGING
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
RaPaX™ maintains an immutable, append-only Audit Log.

The Audit Log records:
- All payment events and confirmations
- All fingerprinting events
- All delivery events and download activity
- All product changes by the operator

The Audit Log is stored locally on your infrastructure.
It is not transmitted to Archer Chain Analytics™.
The Audit Log is not a court-certified forensic record
without independent expert verification.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
7. NO PLATFORM CONTROL
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
RaPaX™ is a sovereign platform. Archer Chain Analytics™:

- Does not control, monitor, or access your store.
- Cannot delist, suspend, or ban your products.
- Cannot alter your pricing or terms.
- Cannot revoke access to your files or data.
- Has no authority over transactions between you and
  your Buyers.

You are the sole operator of your RaPaX™ deployment.
